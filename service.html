A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Does Ateamdata manufacture servers?+

No. Ateamdata focuses on sourcing third-party server platforms and systems integration. We help match manufacturer hardware to your requirements.

Are the listed systems in stock?+

The catalog shows platform options. Availability, configuration, pricing and lead time are confirmed for each inquiry.

Can I request a specific configuration?+

Yes. Send the model, GPU configuration, quantity and any memory, storage or networking requirements. We will review the requested configuration with you.

What warranty or support is included?+

Coverage depends on the equipment, supplier terms and service package. The applicable warranty and support scope are confirmed in your quotation.
