How much GPU memory does your workload need?+

Consider model weights, context length, batch size and training state. Share these details when requesting a configuration.

What does your environment support?+

Review available rack space, electrical capacity, cooling and network fabric with your infrastructure team before choosing the server.

What else should be compared?+

Include software compatibility, system memory, storage, networking, delivery timeline and support scope in the decision.
