About Ateamdata

ATeamData is an independent server brand that focuses on providing high-performance, customized computing solutions to meet the diverse needs of different industries for servers. Our products cover liquid-cooled servers, computing power servers, and AI scheduling software, and are committed to improving data center energy efficiency and optimizing computing resource management, especially in industries such as carriers, finance, education, and artificial intelligence.

As an industry-leading innovative server brand, Ateamdata provides customers with flexible and efficient computing support with deep technology accumulation and keen insight into user needs. Whether it's standard or highly customized solutions, Ateamdata delivers superior performance through cutting-edge technology and exquisite design to meet the growing computing needs of enterprises, and demonstrates advantages in resource scheduling and sustainability, helping customers seize opportunities in digital transformation.
